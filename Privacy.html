Privacy Policy for PetPulse Log

Last Updated: October 19, 2026

Welcome to PetPulse Log (operated under the package name com.sndrcalder39.petpulselog). Your privacy and your pet's health information safety are deeply important to us. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information inside our mobile testing framework.

1. Information We Collect

PetPulse Log is built to operate primarily as a client-side data logger. Vitals logs, pet name tracking metrics, and custom metrics are recorded locally within your device's native sandbox or browser memory profile (local storage). We do not run external tracking cookies.

2. Beta Sign-Up and Contact Data

When registering your interest for our Private Beta Testing program via our landing page, you voluntarily share your name and email address. This contact database is retained strictly for distributing private internal test links and service notifications. We do not distribute your contact details to third-party brokers.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your log history is cached inside your device local memory storage profile, clearing your browser data or deleting the mobile application directory will automatically clear all your logged internal histories. If you want to scrub your email record from our private beta testing database roster, please email us directly to request administrative removal.

4. App Store and Platform Tracking

As a mobile application deployed for beta testing via the Google Play Console, Google may capture technical crash logs, performance diagnostics, and installation metrics to aid debugging. Please consult the Google Play Services privacy policy regarding how automated infrastructure data is regulated.

5. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding our privacy rules, please open an issue tracking request on our repository space or reach out directly via your private developer account contact line.
